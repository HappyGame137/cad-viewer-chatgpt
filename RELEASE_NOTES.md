# CAD Viewer 0.4.0 — edge and face measurements, multiple part selection

Edges mode selects visible circular rims, lines, arcs and general curves. Circles report centre and diameter, lines report length and endpoints, arcs report radius, length, angle, centre and endpoints. A cross marks the circle centre. Circular interiors can select their rim; occluded or hidden features cannot be picked through another surface. Measurements follow the original assembly coordinates and support mm/cm/in and English/Chinese switching.

Faces mode highlights the selected surface and reports mesh area and area centroid. Planes add a normal; recognised cylindrical faces add diameter, axis direction and axial extent. Other curved faces report bounds without inventing a constant normal. STEP uses original face ranges; STL combines adjacent coplanar triangles. Section faces are measurable and obsolete cap selections clear when the cut moves. All reported values remain mesh approximations, rather than exact B-rep metrology.

View now offers a checkbox list with select-all and clear-selection controls. Any combination of parts can remain visible; dimensions update to their combined original bounds and annotations follow part visibility. Measurement modes are mutually exclusive, and clearing or switching off removes their annotations.

Validation: 26 automated tests cover conversion, protocol, units, circle/arc fitting, line extraction, ellipse rejection, face grouping, face area and centroids, cylinder identification, picking/occlusion, localization and filled sections. Manual browser checks cover known 6 mm and 10 mm circles, a 22 mm line, a 90-degree R10 arc, planar and cylindrical faces, square-unit conversion, multiple part visibility, annotation cleanup and section measurements. Native file-entry UI rendering remains a separate verification item.

Dense meshes have documented feature-analysis limits, with explicit triangle fallback for face measurements. The compact bilingual UI and filled sections are retained. No additional runtime dependencies were introduced. The generic measure-edges.step sample and screenshots contain no user project data; private drawings and assembly screenshots are excluded. Third-party versions and the matching OCCT/importer source archive are unchanged. This is a community beta, not an approved OpenAI directory listing.
